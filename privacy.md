# Privacy Policy

YouTube Clips App is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

## What the app accesses

The app asks for one permission: youtube.upload. It uses this permission only to upload videos to the channel of the signed-in owner.

## Data

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data. The sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to third parties other than Google and YouTube, which receive the uploaded videos.

## Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
